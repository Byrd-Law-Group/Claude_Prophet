# Cookies Policy

_Last updated: September 2026_

This page explains how the 6170 Properties LLC website uses cookies and similar
technologies. In plain terms: **we do not use advertising or cross-site tracking
cookies, and we do not build a profile of you.**

## What cookies are

Cookies are small text files a website can store in your browser. They are
often used to remember settings or to track people across different sites. Some
are "strictly necessary" — needed for a feature you asked for to work at all —
while others are used for analytics, advertising, or tracking.

## What this site uses

- **No advertising or tracking cookies.** We run no ad networks, no social-media
  pixels, and no cross-site trackers.
- **Anti-spam protection (strictly necessary).** Our contact forms use
  [Cloudflare Turnstile](https://www.cloudflare.com/products/turnstile/), a
  privacy-friendly alternative to CAPTCHAs, to confirm a real person is
  submitting the form. To run that check, Turnstile may set a strictly necessary
  cookie or read limited technical signals from your browser. It is used only to
  keep spam and abuse off our forms — never for advertising or to track you
  across other websites.
- **Analytics (if any) are cookieless.** If we measure site traffic, we use
  Cloudflare Web Analytics, which does not use cookies and does not track you
  across other websites.

We do not use cookies to identify you personally, and we do not sell or share
any cookie data.

## Why you won't see a cookie consent banner

Under privacy laws such as the EU/UK ePrivacy rules, consent is required for
cookies that are **not** strictly necessary — for example, advertising or
analytics cookies. Because this site sets only strictly necessary anti-spam
protection and uses cookieless analytics, there are no non-essential cookies to
consent to, so no cookie banner is shown.

## Managing cookies

You can block or delete cookies at any time in your browser settings. If you
block the strictly necessary anti-spam cookie, our contact forms may not work,
but you can still reach us directly by email at
[team@6170properties.com](mailto:team@6170properties.com).
