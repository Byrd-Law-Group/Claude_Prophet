# Behind on property taxes? You have options — and we're one of them.

If you got a letter from us, it's because the county records show your property
taxes are past due. That's a stressful thing to open. This page explains what's
happening, what we can offer, and what we are **not** — in plain language, with
no pressure.

## What happens if the taxes stay unpaid

When property taxes go unpaid, the county doesn't just wait forever. It can sell
the unpaid tax debt (a "tax lien") to a private buyer, or it can start a
foreclosure and sell the property itself at a public auction.

Here's the part most people don't hear until it's too late: a property sold at a
county auction usually brings in far less than it's worth — and often far less
than you could get by selling it yourself first. Acting early almost always
leaves you better off than waiting for a sale.

## What we offer

If it makes sense for you to sell, here's what working with us looks like:

- **A fair cash offer** for your property, based on its condition and the local
  market.
- **We pay the delinquent taxes and the closing costs** out of the sale — you
  don't bring money to the table to clear them.
- **We close on your timeline** — quickly if you need it, or later if you need
  more time to move.
- **No repairs and no cleanout.** Take what you want and leave the rest. We buy
  properties as-is.
- **No obligation.** Talking to us costs nothing and commits you to nothing.

## What we are not

We want to be clear about who we are, because a lot of letters land in your
mailbox at a time like this:

- **We are not the county.** We can't change what you owe, and we can't grant a
  payment plan.
- **We are not a collection agency.** We are not trying to collect a debt from
  you.
- **We are not a lender.** We don't offer loans or refinancing.

One honest limit: **we cannot stop a scheduled sale unless we actually close
before it happens.** If a sale date is coming, the sooner we talk, the more we
can do.

## Talk to us

Tell us a little about your situation and the best way to reach you. There's no
cost and no obligation — we'll simply explain your options, including the ones
that don't involve selling to us.
