# Facing foreclosure? You have options — and time matters.

If you're behind on your mortgage or you've received foreclosure papers, it can
feel like the walls are closing in. They're not — you still have choices, and
the earlier you act, the more of them you have. This page explains what's
happening and how we may be able to help, in plain language and with no
pressure.

## What happens in a foreclosure

In Ohio, most foreclosures go through the courts. Your lender files a lawsuit,
and if the court rules against you, the property is ordered sold at a county
**sheriff's sale**. Depending on what's owed, you could even be left owing money
after the sale (a "deficiency").

Here's what many people don't realize until it's late: a property sold at a
sheriff's sale usually brings in far less than it's worth — often less than you
could get by selling it yourself first. Selling before the sale, on your own
terms, almost always leaves you in a better position than letting the auction
happen.

## What we offer

If selling makes sense for you, here's what working with us looks like:

- **A fair cash offer** for your home, based on its condition and the local
  market.
- **We can close before the sheriff's sale** when there's enough time — which
  is why reaching out early matters so much.
- **We handle the payoff and the paperwork.** Where possible, the sale pays off
  what's owed and clears the liens, so you can move on.
- **No repairs and no cleanout.** We buy as-is. Take what you want and leave the
  rest.
- **No obligation.** Talking to us costs nothing and commits you to nothing.

## What we are not

There are a lot of foreclosure "rescue" pitches out there, so we want to be
clear about who we are:

- **We are not your lender** and we're not the court. We can't change your loan
  or grant a modification.
- **We are not a rescue service** promising to magically stop your foreclosure.
  Anyone who guarantees that — especially for an upfront fee — should be treated
  with caution.
- **We are not attorneys or housing counselors.** For legal advice or a full
  review of your options, please talk to one (see the note below).

One honest limit: **we cannot stop a scheduled sheriff's sale unless we actually
close before it happens.** If a sale date is set, the sooner we talk, the more
we can do.
