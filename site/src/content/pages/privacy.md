# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: September 2026_

6170 Properties LLC ("we," "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains
what information this website collects and how we use it.

## What we collect

When you submit a form on this site, we collect the information you choose to
enter — such as your name, phone number, email address, property address, best
time to call, and any message you write. To reduce spam, our forms also use a
hidden anti-spam field and Cloudflare Turnstile, a privacy-friendly challenge
that helps confirm you are a real person.

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies on this site. If we use analytics,
it is Cloudflare Web Analytics, which is cookieless and does not track you
across other websites.

## How we use it

The information you submit is **emailed to us** so we can respond to your
inquiry. We use it only to contact you and to evaluate whether we can help with
your property.

## What we do not do

We do **not** sell, rent, or trade your information. We do not share it with
third parties except as needed to operate this site (for example, our email
provider and Cloudflare) or as required by law.

## How to request deletion

You can ask us to delete the information you submitted at any time. Email
[notices@6170properties.com](mailto:notices@6170properties.com) with the details
you provided and we will remove it from our records.

## Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to
[notices@6170properties.com](mailto:notices@6170properties.com).
