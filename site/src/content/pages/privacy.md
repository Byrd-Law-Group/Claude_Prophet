# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: September 2026_

6170 Properties LLC ("we," "us"), an Ohio limited liability company, respects
your privacy. This policy explains what information this website collects, why,
and what choices you have. It applies only to this website.

## What we collect

When you submit a form on this site, we collect the information you choose to
enter — such as your name, phone number, email address, property address, best
time to call, and any message you write. Only your name and one way to reach you
(a phone number or email) are required; everything else is optional.

We ask only for what we need to respond to you. We do not require you to create
an account, and we do not collect payment information through this site.

To reduce spam, our forms also use a hidden anti-spam field and
[Cloudflare Turnstile](https://www.cloudflare.com/products/turnstile/), a
privacy-friendly challenge that helps confirm you are a real person. As with any
website, our hosting provider (Cloudflare) may process standard technical data
such as your IP address to deliver and secure the site.

We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies on this site. If we use
analytics, it is Cloudflare Web Analytics, which is cookieless and does not track
you across other websites. See our [Cookies Policy](/cookies) for details.

## How we use it

The information you submit is **emailed to us** so we can respond to your
inquiry. We use it only to contact you and to evaluate whether we can help with
your property. We do not use it to make automated decisions about you, and we do
not use it for advertising.

## What we do not do

We do **not** sell, rent, or trade your information. We do not share it with
third parties except as needed to operate this site (for example, our email
provider, [Resend](https://resend.com), and Cloudflare) or as required by law.

## How long we keep it

We keep the information you submit only as long as we need it to respond to your
inquiry and for our reasonable business and legal record-keeping, and then we
delete it. You can ask us to delete it sooner at any time (see below).

## Your choices

- **Access or correction.** You can ask us what information of yours we hold, or
  ask us to correct it.
- **Deletion.** You can ask us to delete the information you submitted at any
  time. Email
  [team@6170properties.com](mailto:team@6170properties.com) with the details you
  provided and we will remove it from our records.
- **Contact preferences.** Tell us how and when you prefer to be contacted, and
  we will respect that.

We honor these requests regardless of where you live. Depending on your state or
country, you may have additional rights under local law; email us and we will
help.

## Children

This site is intended for adults dealing with property matters. It is not
directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the
"Last updated" date above.

## Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to
[team@6170properties.com](mailto:team@6170properties.com).
