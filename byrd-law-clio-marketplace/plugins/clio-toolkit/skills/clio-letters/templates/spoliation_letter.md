# Spoliation / Evidence-Preservation Letter

Used for serious/commercial-truck cases to demand preservation of evidence (ELD,
telematics, dashcam, driver qualification file, etc.). Attorney-driven — the firm's
MVA workflow routes this to the supervising attorney. Fill parties; the attorney
sets scope.

---

{{today_date}}

VIA [CONFIRM: certified mail / email]

[CONFIRM: Recipient — motor carrier / owner / insurer name and address]

**RE:**  Preservation of Evidence — {{client_name}}
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Date of Loss: [CONFIRM: date of loss]
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Location: [CONFIRM: collision location]

Dear Sir or Madam:

This firm represents {{client_name}} in connection with the above collision. You
are hereby placed on notice of your legal duty to **preserve all evidence** that
may be relevant to this matter. Do not alter, destroy, delete, or discard any of
the following, whether in paper or electronic form:

- The vehicle(s) involved and their electronic control module / event data recorder data;
- Electronic logging device (ELD) and hours-of-service records;
- Telematics, GPS, and dashcam/onboard camera data;
- Driver qualification file, training records, and disciplinary history;
- Maintenance, inspection, and repair records for the vehicle;
- Dispatch records, bills of lading, and trip documents;
- Any post-collision drug/alcohol testing records; and
- Any communications concerning the driver, vehicle, or collision.

[CONFIRM: additional items per attorney]

Failure to preserve this evidence may result in a claim for spoliation and
associated sanctions. Please confirm in writing that these items have been
preserved.

Sincerely,

{{responsible_attorney}}
[Firm Name]

---

**Guardrails:** Attorney sets the preservation scope and approves before sending.
Do not narrow or broaden the standard list without attorney direction. Confirm the
recipient and loss details.
