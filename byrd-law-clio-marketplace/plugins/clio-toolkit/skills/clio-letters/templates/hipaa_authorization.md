# HIPAA Authorization for Release of Protected Health Information

A form authorization for the client to sign. This is a legal form — do NOT alter the
authorization language; only fill the party fields. State-specific requirements vary;
the attorney confirms the firm's approved form.

---

**AUTHORIZATION FOR RELEASE OF PROTECTED HEALTH INFORMATION**

I, **{{client_name}}** (DOB: [CONFIRM: date of birth]), authorize:

**[CONFIRM: Provider / custodian of records name and address]**

to disclose the protected health information described below to:

**[Firm Name]**, attorneys for the patient
[Firm address]

**Information to be disclosed:** All medical records, itemized billing statements,
radiology/imaging, physical therapy notes, and related records concerning treatment
from [CONFIRM: date range — typically the date of loss to present].

**Purpose:** Legal representation of the patient in a personal-injury claim.

**Expiration:** This authorization expires [CONFIRM: one year from signing / on
resolution of the claim], unless revoked earlier in writing.

I understand I may revoke this authorization in writing at any time except to the
extent action has already been taken in reliance on it. I understand that
information disclosed pursuant to this authorization may be subject to
re-disclosure and may no longer be protected by federal privacy regulations.

Patient signature: ______________________________  Date: ____________

Printed name: {{client_name}}

---

**Guardrails:** Do not change the authorization/revocation language. Fill only the
party, date-range, and provider fields. Client signs; the firm does not sign for the
client. Confirm the provider name/address and DOB with the user.
